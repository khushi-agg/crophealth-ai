# Video demo photos (use in this order)

Each photo was tested on the live app (khushi-agg.github.io/crophealth-ai) and gives the result the video script expects.

| File | Script scene | How to scan it | Result you should see |
|---|---|---|---|
| `1-leaf-scan-tomato-late-blight.jpg` | Demo 1: Leaf scan | Leaf photo → tap **Tomato** → Choose from gallery | "Possible disease found · Late blight", Confidence 99% |
| `2-not-sure-desk.jpg` | Demo 2: Honest when unsure | Scan another leaf → Choose from gallery | "Not sure — please retake" · "We can't find much leaf in this photo." |
| `3-drone-field-73m.jpg` | Demo 4: Drone field analysis | Drone field map → Upload drone photos | "2 problem zones · 19% of field flagged · ≈ 2,122 m²" |
